One frame, as Check's and Prepare's: the heading, the About panel, the Go live step bar, the step's content and one action row (the secondary Stop go-live first, then exactly one primary, or none while there is nothing to do), with the reserved status line under it. No Back: Prepare cannot be redone without stopping. The content is one impact preview, as of a browser-local time: whether the campaign becomes active now or is scheduled, the Families with and without an eligible email address, and the Family and report emails due at once, with the combined and skipped counts in a disclosure. A line names what changed since go-live started (the start time passed, or the links were prepared again on changed ParishSoft data). The step bar, content and row are one region (#go-live-confirm) that updates itself (live-status-v1.js) every 30 seconds while go-live is in progress, so a change made elsewhere shows here; there is no Refresh link (#1586). Once the step is no longer the one in progress (the links are being prepared again, go-live was stopped, or Production was confirmed elsewhere) the region settles and its primary, marked data-live-follow, opens the page that now applies. A reload draws the same state. ``confirm`` is go_live_confirm's Confirm: ready, problems, hold_ended, closed, moved, stopped, not_started or confirmed; ``stage`` is the Prepare read it comes from (its Stop go-live and Refresh and prepare again controls). {% endcomment %} {% block title %}{% translate "Go live" %}{% endblock %} {% block head %}{% if not admin_chrome.campaign_wizard %}{% endif %}{% endblock %} {% block content %}
