Every time is shown in the browser's time zone. Why sends are waiting and the mail senders are one tile (decision 1 of 2026-10-13): a stopped sender is the reason sends wait. The panel shows the data and the links that act on it; why each wait happens is in a toggletip beside it, and the longer explanation in "About this page" (ui-conventions "Page help", slice 3 of #1214). The mail consumers come as health.sender_groups: one line for both while they show the same state, one line each when they differ, so neither list says one reason twice. Toggletip ids carry the group's key, so they stay unique on the page; the polled copy is an inert template, so its ids never clash. {% endcomment %}
