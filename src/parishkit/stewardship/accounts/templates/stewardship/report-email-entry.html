One form in the spec's order: Name and Subject, Recipients, Schedule with As of, Blocks with Send only when there is something to report, and the Introduction. Its button (Add the report email on New, Review changes on Edit) reviews the change in the shared review dialog (UX conventions, "Review dialog"): a field refusal answers this page again with the errors at their fields; an accepted review opens the dialog, whose Apply confirms here and returns to Report emails. Review changes waits for a real change (data-require-change) and every required field (data-require-complete), with its reason after it. Preview beside it (render=yes, #2035) posts the unsaved form too, once it is complete but whether or not it changed (data-change-exempt), and opens its own dialog, "Preview — not sent", which has no Apply (data-review-open on the button). Send a test to me beside it (test=yes, #2036) is held by the same gate and posts the unsaved form in place from the page script (data-review-skip, so no dialog opens); the reserved status line under the buttons says "Test sent to you at 9:42 AM." or why it was refused. On New, Start from (Blank, Daily report or Weekly report) sits above the form, drawn in the shared segmented style with three radios that are never posted; report-email-editor-v1.js fills every field from the chosen starting point (report-starting-points, report_email_editor.starting_points from campaigns/report_presets.py), asking first in report-start-replace when that would replace typing. A refused Add (a bound form) is drawn with no choice made and counts as typed (data-start-changed). Sends (Once | Repeating), As of and Frequency are stored two-way settings, each a segmented pair of radios (components/choice-pair.html; UX conventions, "Binary choices"). The Introduction is the shared HTML and plain-text editor every authored email part uses (components/html-content-editor.html). report-email-editor-v1.js runs the recipients picker (a text box with the eligible users and groups as its suggestion list; Add makes a chip holding a hidden recipient field), the Blocks list (Add a block, Move up, Move down and Remove, each in place, focus kept and the new position announced; each row holds its hidden block field, in order), the Basic and Advanced modes of a repeating schedule (ParishEditorMode), the live Next sends table (a read of schedule_preview, redrawn in a region that keeps its height) and the line that a repeating report's days are the campaign's, shown when this browser's zone keeps a different clock from the campaign's on the campaign's days (data-campaign-days, compared over data-campaign-start and data-campaign-end; another name for the same zone is no difference). The review dialog's "Send after the campaign ends" tick is its Apply form's own field, held by the shared review gate (report-email-review.html). Dates and times are typed in this browser's zone (data-browser-zone); on an unbound Edit page ui-v1.js fills the time (and a one-time date) from the moment drawn (data-schedule-local), as Edit scheduled email does. A repeating report's days are the campaign's days. A one-time send's "time has passed" (or "happens twice") refusal concerns its date and its time, so it marks both and is said once, after the time (date_time_error); an edit of either clears both (UX conventions, "Field errors and live checks"). On these days and the Participation charts block's Charts need one box ticked (data-require-one): unticking the last says the server's words in the group's reserved error line at once (ui-v1.js sayRequireOne, #2009), and the complete gate holds Review until one is ticked. On Edit of a repeating report, the Next sends table's ticked sends go to Skip selected sends or Send selected sends (report-skip-form, one review dialog, which redraws the table in place), the report's past sends follow the form, and Delete has a section of its own whose review returns to the list. {% endcomment %}
