Review changes posts here without a page load (data-review-open, UX conventions "Review dialog", #750): while the answer is prepared the form is inert and busy; an accepted review of the whole set of schedules, as every schedule change has, opens the shared review dialog below with schedule-review.html, whose Apply posts to Dates and mail schedules and follows the change, then returns there, where the status line says what was applied. A review refused for the values posted keeps the dialog shut: ui-v1.js marks each field and shows the summary from this page drawn again with the errors. Any other refusal (a stale page, a read-only schedule, a refused Apply) shows inside the dialog. The button stays unavailable until the form differs from what the page was drawn with (data-require-change, #750): on Edit, the saved schedule as the page script shows it in this browser's zone; on New, the blank form. Its hint keeps its space, so it never moves the page. The repeat summary reserves its lines above the button and the date list sits below it, so neither moves the button. Send dates and times are shown and typed in the browser's time zone (#558): ui-v1.js fills the hidden zone field (data-browser-zone) and names the zone in the note, and on an unbound Edit page fills the fields from the saved moment in data-schedule-local, which become the values the change gate compares with. Without a zone, data-zone-missing shows and the button stays unavailable. The form carries the campaign dates and time zone (data-window-start, -end and -zone) for the send date's window check (#1787): ui-v1.js refuses a send date and time outside the campaign at the date, as the server would, and holds the button meanwhile. Both offer "Edit this schedule's email" (schedule-email.html, #446): a changed email is reviewed with the schedule and saved as a new email that only this schedule (on New, every reminder its repeat adds) sends. On New the button reads "Add the scheduled email" (the action's own label, UX conventions "A confirmation when Review would only repeat the page", #1356). A schedule that sends once, with no edited email, is answered with a confirmation that repeats none of the form's values and names its own question and sentence (data-review-title, data-review-describedby); any other New (recurring, a digest, a Repeat, an edited email) keeps the full review under this dialog's own title. A Repeat is also remembered as a reminder series, and Edit of a series' reminder shows "Part of" its rule with the Change scope (schedule-series-scope.html, drawn by schedule-row.html, #469); the review then lists the series it keeps or changes. Edit opened for one send of a digest from the Campaign calendar (?send=, skip_send, #1658) offers "This email only" (scope=send, the default, with that send's slot in the hidden send field), which skips just that send, and "The whole digest" (scope=this); otherwise a digest's Delete is the whole digest alone. Edit has Delete in a section of its own after the form (#1656): a second form, posting action=preview to Delete scheduled emails, that opens its own review dialog (schedule-delete-review) with the server's review of removing this schedule, or with "The whole series" chosen for a series' reminder, every unsent reminder of its series. Apply confirms on Dates and mail schedules like Review changes, then returns to list_url (the list, or the calendar month the page was opened from). Its one help line is always shown, so choosing a scope moves nothing. Inside the campaign wizard (admin_chrome.campaign_wizard.direct, #1404 PR 4b) the page draws neither its own button nor Cancel, nor the review dialog, nor Delete: the form is marked data-direct-save and posts action=save with the stepper's request key, from the wizard's action row's Save and go back (campaign-step-actions.html), which the same change gate and zone check hold, named by the same hint; ui-v1.js follows the saved change and opens Dates and mail schedules again, its status line saying what was saved. {% endcomment %}{% with direct=admin_chrome.campaign_wizard.direct %}
