{% extends 'stewardship/admin-base.html' %}{% load i18n stewardship %} {% block title %}{% translate "Dates and mail schedules" %}{% endblock %} {% block content %}{% comment %} The date-change review (schedule_views._page): the proposed campaign dates (from Campaign settings, or a live end date's stranded mailings) with every schedule's editor, reviewed and applied together. Review changes posts here without a page load (data-review-open, UX conventions "Review dialog", #750): while the answer is prepared the form is inert and busy; an accepted review opens the shared review dialog below with schedule-review.html, whose Apply posts the signed preview to Dates and mail schedules, follows the change and then returns to the list, where the status line says what was applied. A review refused for the values posted keeps the dialog shut: ui-v1.js marks each field, shows the summary and opens the editors the answer opened. Any other refusal (a stale page, sends in progress, a refused Apply) shows inside the dialog. Review changes stays unavailable until the form differs from the saved values (data-require-change): proposed dates are drawn as the fields' values but carry the saved ones (data-saved-value, ScheduleWindow), so the page opens with Review available and reverting the dates makes it unavailable again. A blank added schedule is no change. Its hint keeps its space, so it never moves the page. Send dates and times are shown and typed in the browser's time zone (#558), as on the first-campaign step: ui-v1.js fills the hidden zone field (data-browser-zone), names the zone in the note, and fills each saved row from its data-schedule-local moment, making those values the fields' defaults, so the change gate compares with the saved schedules as this browser shows them. Without a zone, data-zone-missing shows and Review changes stays unavailable. The campaign dates are calendar dates, and its time zone is a setting; neither is converted. {% endcomment %}
