Without a failure the button shows only with ``idle_action`` (the ParishSoft page); the Admin home page already has its own "Refresh now" link. A failed full reload is described separately from the incremental updates (every 15 minutes or hourly), which keep Family contact changes arriving (#219). With several full-refresh times a day (#465) the notice names the most recent reload rather than "the nightly" one. The first two lines state the data age and the connection (#510): "data as of" is the last full refresh or a later quick update that changed records; the connection line says whether ParishSoft answers. A scheduled full refresh past the lateness margin is named as late (with the run button beside it when none is running), or as held while a bulk send holds it. Every time is shown in the browser's time zone (data-local-instant). {% endcomment %}
