Its step bar and list are the setup wizard's (stepper-steps.html, #1402). Before any campaign exists, base.html also draws it on Create the campaign (campaign_wizard.creation, #1404 PR 3): step 1 current, every later step unavailable, no load notice and no shared action row. The page naming a creation request (?request=, while pending or after a failed Apply) keeps it until the campaign exists, since no campaign exists yet; once one does, that address draws none. Its summary names the next step that is not done yet, as text, and offers Leave campaign setup (Home); the moving controls, Back and the one primary Continue, are the action row at the bottom of the page (campaign-step-actions.html, #1404), so leaving a page never saves or discards anything. It is an in-place region, so the redraw after a review dialog's Apply (from the page's data-refresh-url) or an in-place refresh shows the new completion marks without a reload; a reload shows the same. After it, outside the region: the reserved status line a step reached from a review dialog's Apply names what was applied in (Create the campaign's, #1404), and, until the campaign has its Families, the offer to load its ParishSoft data, the line saying it is loading, or, once the newest load failed, that line with a link to its Background task and Try again (#1404 PR 5), so a failed load never looks as if none was started.
