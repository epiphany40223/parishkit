Save is unavailable until the rows differ from the saved list (data-require-change, #921), with its hint after it. Save posts the form without a page load (data-review-open, UX conventions "Review dialog", #750): an accepted review opens the shared review dialog below with share-options-review.html, a compact review of only what changed (#1356), whose Apply posts the signed preview here, follows the change and, once it is applied, redraws the form's region from this page's address (the applied list and a fresh blank row); the status line under the form says what was applied. A Review refused for a row's value keeps the dialog shut: ui-v1.js marks each field and shows the summary from this page drawn again. Any other refusal (a stale page, an out-of-date preview, a draft that can no longer change) shows inside the dialog. Inside the campaign wizard (admin_chrome.campaign_wizard.direct, #1404 PR 4) the page draws no Save of its own: the form is marked data-direct-save and posts action=save with the stepper's request key, from the wizard's action row's Save and continue (campaign-step-actions.html); ui-v1.js follows the saved change and opens the next step. Save (or Save and continue) also waits for every label to be valid (data-require-complete, #1577): a label's shared format rule (web/field_rules.py) is checked as it is left, its message in the field's reserved line, and the hint after the form says what is missing; so does a short label another option has (#1569, ui-v1.js data-distinct), marked as it is typed. The hint keeps its line while empty (data-complete-hint="reserve"), so its words coming and going move nothing. {% endcomment %}
