It is always drawn, and its button follows the polled state: greyed with its reason while no refusal can be accepted. ``mirrored`` (the polled copy) leaves the button's CSRF token empty. Its forms post to the System health page itself, which answers with this page; ui-v1.js swaps this region (#accept-change) in place. ``accept`` holds the step's result: ``preview`` (a system_health.AcceptPreview: the refusal's counts, counts only, and the signed binding), ``requested`` (the acceptance just recorded, or the first one when the confirmation was repeated) with ``refresh`` (the full refresh requested, or None), or ``refused`` (why the step was refused: ``busy`` is "this changed since you looked", with the step-up for a stale sign-in). Under each count that fell too far the preview lists its example Families (PR 5c: name and Family DUID, Administrators only), or says none are available; they are part of the preview, drawn with it, so nothing appears or moves afterwards. Without a preview, the button is shown in a data-live-mirror-target that the polled region keeps current. The preview names the refusal it reviews (data-live-keep-for); once a poll shows another (or none) in the region's data-live-key, live-status-v1.js marks it changed on its reserved line and holds Confirm. Confirm waits for the tick box (data-acknowledgment); the server checks the box and every other condition again. {% endcomment %}
