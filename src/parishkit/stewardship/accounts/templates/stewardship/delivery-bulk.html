The panel is drawn only while some email qualifies (bulk_overview, which also lists each action's email types with their counts), or while it answers a bulk action. Each action is its own form, shown only when it applies, so no field appears or disappears as another is chosen. Preview is unavailable until the note (and, to record emails as not sent, the checkbox) is filled in (data-require-complete, #553). Preview and Confirm happen in place (#532): each form posts to this page, which answers with its #bulk-review region holding the preview (with Confirm), the result (with Continue while a batch is left) or the refusal. Only that region and the table are swapped, so the forms keep their typing and nothing above the region moves. Editing a form again withdraws its preview (data-review-of), so Confirm only ever applies what is shown; the server's signed preview stays the authority for which emails change. {% endcomment %}{% if bulk_overview or bulk_review or bulk_result or bulk_errors %}
