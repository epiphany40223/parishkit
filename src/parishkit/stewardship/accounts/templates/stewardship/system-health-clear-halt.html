The #clear-halt in-place region is always drawn, so a step's answer always has a region to swap in, but it shows nothing while no mail sender is halted: without a step result it holds only a data-live-mirror-target that the polled region keeps current with system-health-clear-halt-offer.html. Its forms post to the System health page itself, which answers with this page; ui-v1.js swaps this region in place, and data-live-recheck reads the status again at once, so the tile's words follow without waiting for the next poll. ``clear`` holds the step's result: ``preview`` (a mail_halt_clear.ClearPreview: when the halt began and its kind, the waiting and unsettled emails, whether delivery is also paused, and the signed binding), ``cleared`` (the clear signal just recorded, or the first one when Confirm was repeated), or ``refused`` (``busy``: a check no longer holds or the halt changed, in the service's words ``refusal_text``; ``denied``; ``invalid``). The preview names the halt it reviews (data-live-keep-for); once a poll shows another halt (or none) in the region's data-live-key, live-status-v1.js marks it changed on its reserved line and holds both buttons. The mailbox check's outcome and Confirm follow the poll in place (system-health-clear-halt-check.html). No step needs a recent sign-in (#1547). {% endcomment %}
