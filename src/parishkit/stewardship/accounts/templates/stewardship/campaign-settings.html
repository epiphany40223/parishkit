{% extends 'stewardship/admin-base.html' %}{% load i18n static stewardship %} {% block title %}{% translate "Campaign settings" %}{% endblock %} {% block content %}{% comment %} Campaign settings (campaign_views.campaign_settings): one editor (#1119). Every setting is drawn once, in the same place in every campaign state, by campaign-fields.html: open where it can change now, otherwise disabled where it stands with the reason in its "i" tip (UX conventions, Conditional fields), never in a line of its own. There is one Review changes, unavailable until the form holds a real change (data-require-change, #921), with its hint after it; on a draft it also waits for a complete form (#563), and on a live campaign for the end date's live check (#944). Its hint lines keep their space, so nothing moves under the pointer. Review posts the form without a page load (data-review-open, UX conventions "Review dialog", #750): an accepted review opens the shared review dialog below with campaign-settings-review.html, whose Apply posts the signed preview here, follows the change and, once it is applied, redraws the form's region in place; the status line under the form says what was applied. A review refused for the values posted keeps the dialog shut: ui-v1.js marks each field and shows the summary from this page drawn again. Any other refusal (a stale page, background work, a refused Apply) shows inside the dialog. A draft's change of its dates alone leads to Dates and mail schedules' combined review instead (the form is marked data-review-redirects). Copy campaign is greyed out until the single-campaign change (#145) removes it (navigation rule 10); the server refuses it too. The body links no page the menu offers (#1118). Content history is not a menu entry and this page is its breadcrumb parent, so it stays linked here, in every campaign state (navigation rule 1). {% endcomment %}
