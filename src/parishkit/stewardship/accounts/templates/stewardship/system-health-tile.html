{% load i18n stewardship %}{% comment %} One System health tile (admin-portal spec, "Health tiles", #1214), in two parts so the tile grid can place them apart. First a section holding a visually hidden h2 (heading navigation reaches each tile) and the summary row, a disclosure button (aria-expanded, aria-controls) rather than a native details element, whose content could not be laid out apart from its summary. Then the details: a full-width band that the grid places after the row holding the tile, so opening a tile moves nothing beside it or above it (ui-conventions "No layout shift"). Working tiles start closed, Warning and Error tiles open (``tile.open``); system-health-v1.js opens only the tile the address's anchor names, and the Administrator decides after that, by the toggle or a click on the open band's blank space: a poll never opens or closes a tile. The summary row's words, the problems and the panel content are data-live-mirror-targets that follow the poll. The open state and the action regions are not, so an open tile, an open preview and a ticked box survive every poll. The problems and panel targets wait while the pointer is over the grid (data-live-mirror-hold), because their height can change. Details come in a fixed order: the problems, then the tile's action region, then the panel content and its links. A page-load tile (``tile.loaded``) has no polled parts. {% endcomment %}
