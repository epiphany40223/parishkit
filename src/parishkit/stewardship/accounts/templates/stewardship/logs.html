{% translate "What the system did (operational entries) and who changed what (the audit trail), together, newest first unless you sort by Time the other way. Entries hold identifiers and counts only, never names, amounts or credentials; an actor's address is looked up for display and is not part of the entry. {% translate "Times show to the second in your computer's time zone, followed by that zone's short name, so you can compare them with times recorded elsewhere. The From and Through dates are whole days in your computer's time zone too: From starts at midnight, and Through includes its whole day. The Show choices pick which kinds of entry are listed: the five operational levels and audit records, which have no level and their own clipboard icon. Tick Audit record alone to see only the audit trail, or leave it unticked to see only operational entries; tick at least one choice. The Level column shows each entry's icon, the same one beside its choice; point at an icon to see its name." %}

{% translate "Activity lists a fixed group of entries together, so you never type internal type names one at a time. Sign-in activity shows portal sign-ins (Administrators, Staff and Ministry leaders), sign-ins confirmed again for a protected action, sign-outs, sessions that timed out, were ended because access was removed, or were replaced by a new sign-in or by one carrying changed roles, and refused sign-ins. Configuration changes shows settings changes from request through review to applied, refused or cancelled, mail schedule and live campaign changes, portal user role changes, hosted file changes, stored key replacements and initial setup (starting it, entering and clearing its credentials, and locking it or letting it expire). Family mail shows each outgoing email's steps (sent, retried, refused or delivery unknown), what Administrators decided about them, cleared refused addresses, delivery pauses, test emails and mail provider failures; an email's steps don't record whom it was for, so staff report and alert emails are listed too. ParishSoft refreshes shows full refreshes and quick updates that were accepted, rejected, replaced or turned into a full reload, and their failures and holds; time limits that stopped a refresh are listed under their own types, such as task_timed_out." %}

{% translate "Reports and downloads shows reports and exports requested, made, cancelled or downloaded, from the portal or the command line; opening a report page is not listed. Security events shows settings changes that widened access or replaced the backup encryption key (these are configuration changes too) and their acknowledgements, automation sessions approved, refused or ended, an automation session standing in for a recent Google sign-in, Administrator access or sign-in limiting restored, and the installation's encryption keys changed. Family portal activity shows Families signing in and out, sessions ending, responses, invalid links, Family links prepared or replaced, and the Family portal closed for maintenance." %}

{% translate "Family mail and ParishSoft refreshes include operational entries too, so the levels stay available. Every other group is audit records only: while one is chosen the five levels are greyed out, and Apply waits until Audit record is ticked. The Actor column names the person, and an entry's same-actor icon keeps the group, listing only that person's entries in it. A Google account that Google confirmed but this system refuses (its address is not a user, the person is disabled, or access was removed during the sign-in itself) is named, at most once every ten minutes per account and at most 20 accounts every ten minutes in all; past that, it is recorded like any other refused sign-in. Any other refused sign-in is recorded at most once every five minutes and names no one, because anyone can try to sign in. The Users page links to Sign-in activity, and each user's Edit page links to that person's sign-in activity." %}

{% translate "Search finds entries whose type, its explanation or a recorded detail value contains the words you type, in either case. It does not search actor addresses or the sentence that says what went wrong. A search reads every entry in the date range, so a narrower range answers faster. Ministry lists the entries whose detail names that Ministry's DUID, including a Ministry report download that included it." %}

{% translate "Link to these filters is the page's own address with the type, Ministry, Show and date filters you applied, so you can bookmark it or send it to another Administrator; the address bar follows it too. Search text and identifier filters are never put in a link, because a web address is kept in the server's records and the browser's history. A link's From and Through days are in the time zone of the computer it was made on; the page says so when that differs from yours." %}

{% translate "Each entry's type is followed by a short explanation. An actor shown as Background worker is one of the system's own processes, not a person. The Related column has up to five icons, and an entry shows only the ones that apply to it. The chain link lists the entries from the same task or request. The person lists every entry by the same actor. The target lists the audit records about the same subject. The calendar lists the audit records for the same campaign. The arrow opens the task's own page, where Technical details shows its identifiers. Point at an icon to see its name. This list shows no identifiers; a download of these entries includes them." %}

{% translate "A warning, error or critical entry also says in words what went wrong this time and what happens next. When a problem that raised an alert ends, a Recovered entry says so (or says what still to check); its chain link icon lists the entry that opened the problem." %}
