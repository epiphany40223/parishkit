{% load i18n stewardship %}{% comment %} The step bar and the full step list that the setup wizard's stepper (setup-wizard.html) and the Campaign setup stepper (campaign-stepper.html) share, so the two cannot drift (#1402; ui-conventions "Step bars"). Each bar segment of a step that can be opened is a link to its page, named "Step N of M: label — status" and marked aria-current="step" when it is the current step; the same text shows as a floating tooltip on hover and keyboard focus, which moves nothing. A step that cannot open yet (a missing prerequisite, the ordering rule or the running data load) is not a link: its segment stays focusable but aria-disabled, and its name and tooltip say why ("Not available yet: ..."). Clicking or tapping it only focuses it, which shows that tooltip (ui-v1.js), as the Family bar does. Following a segment saves nothing. The list below repeats the same steps inside a collapsed disclosure. The segment's bar, tap target and tooltip share their CSS with the Family step bar (ui-v1.css).
